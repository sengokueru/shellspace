## Small-buffer performance

At prepare time, blocks below 256 samples select non-uniform convolution with a 256-sample
head; larger blocks retain uniform convolution. Both policies add zero latency.

Ryzen 7 3700U, 48 kHz, quick benchmark (2 seconds, one repetition):

| 64-sample configuration | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| BODY + stereo SPACE | 1.4618 | 0.4887 |
| BODY + True Stereo SPACE | 2.4723 | 0.8037 |

Values are elapsed processing time / audio duration, **not OS CPU percentages**.
These short measurements show roughly a 3x improvement in these configurations,
not a universal speedup or a guaranteed simultaneous-instance count.
512-sample processing retains its previous partition policy.

Impulse comparisons cover 147456 samples at 48 kHz, including the full hall tail.
Maximum absolute differences between 64/512 policies: BODY 0.000001383,
SPACE 0.000001861, True Stereo 0.000001740. Floating-point summation order differs;
the outputs are not bit-identical. IR synthesis itself is unchanged.
